Features

Everything between the camera and the finished file.

From the live viewfinder to a signed, encrypted PDF, without leaving the app.

Live scanner

The page outline is tracked as you aim, and the shot is taken automatically once the page holds still. Torch, tap to focus and scan modes are a tap away; the full-resolution shot is then analysed again.

Scan modes

Batch, ID card (front and back on one sheet at real size) and book, which splits a two-page spread in two.

Edit pages

Crop with draggable corners, a magnifier and snapping. Rotate, retake, reorder, extract pages into a new document or merge documents, with undo.

Sign and mark up

Pen and highlighter, plus saved hand-drawn signatures you place and resize on any page.

Searchable text

Text is recognized on the device in the background. Search finds words inside pages, copy or share a page's text, and PDFs get an invisible text layer.

Export

PDF in Auto, A4, Letter or Legal with margins and three quality levels, AES-256 passwords and watermarks. Or JPEG pages and one long image.

A library that stays tidy

Folders, favorites and search; import photos from your library or existing PDFs, page by page. Pinch to zoom, print, save to Photos, share every page at once. Light and dark themes.

Enhancement

Five looks, one tap each.

Pick a filter and fine-tune brightness and contrast. Every edit is non-destructive, so the original is always one tap away.

Your documents stay on your phone.

Scanning and text recognition run on the device, and documents never leave it unless you share them. Lock the app with a PIN, fingerprint or Face ID; it locks again whenever it leaves the screen.

Privacy policy

What GoScan does with your data

This policy covers the GoScan apps for Android and iOS.

Last updated 19 November 2026.

No account, no server, no tracking

GoScan has no sign-in, no servers of its own, no advertising and no analytics. Its developer receives nothing from the app: no documents, no usage data, no crash reports. Nothing is used to track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

Everything GoScan keeps is stored in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your documents and folders, the page pictures, crops, filters and markup;
  • the text recognized on pages, used for search and searchable PDFs;
  • the signatures you draw;
  • your settings, including the author name written into exported PDFs.

If you set an app lock PIN, only a salted hash of it is kept, in the system's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore-protected storage on Android). Fingerprint and Face ID are checked by the system; GoScan never sees them. Uninstalling GoScan removes all of it. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include this data, depending on your settings.

Camera, photos and files

The camera is used only while you scan, and pictures are processed on the device. Photos and PDFs are read only when you pick them to import. GoScan writes to your photo library only when you choose Save to Photos, and creates files only when you save, share or print one: where they go then is up to you and the app or printer you choose.

Text recognition

Text is recognized on the device: by Apple's Vision framework on iOS, and by Google's ML Kit with its model built into the app on Android. Page pictures and their text are never uploaded.

On Android, ML Kit itself sends Google diagnostic and usage information so Google can maintain it: the device model and Android version, the app's package name and version, a per-installation identifier that does not identify you or your device, and performance and error metrics. It is sent over HTTPS and not shared with third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure. The iOS app sends nothing.

Children, changes and contact

GoScan is not directed at children, and its developer collects no data from anyone. If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/xmhrnx/doclens/issues.
