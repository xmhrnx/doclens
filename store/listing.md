## Description (Google Play full description and App Store description, 4000)

GoScan turns your phone into a document scanner. Point the camera at a page: GoScan
finds its edges as you aim, takes the picture when it's steady, straightens it and
cleans it up into a sharp, white page, ready to share as a PDF.

Your documents stay on your device: no account, no cloud, no ads.

SCAN
• Live page detection: the outline follows the page while you aim
• Automatic capture once the page holds still, or tap the shutter
• Perspective correction recovers the page's true shape
• Batch mode for many pages in a row
• ID card mode: front and back on one sheet, at real size
• Book mode: a two-page spread split into two pages
• Import photos from your gallery and pages from existing PDFs
• Share pictures and PDFs from other apps straight into GoScan

ENHANCE
• Magic Color, No Shadow, Grayscale and Black & White filters
• Removes shadows and uneven lighting
• Brightness and contrast
• Crop with draggable corners, a magnifier and pinch-to-zoom
• Every edit can be changed later: your original photo is kept

TEXT
• Text recognition on the device
• Search finds words inside your pages
• Copy or share a page's text
• Searchable PDFs with an invisible text layer

SIGN AND MARK UP
• Draw your signature once, place it on any page
• Pen and highlighter

EXPORT AND SHARE
• PDF in Auto, A4, Letter or Legal size, with or without margins
• Password-protected PDFs with AES-256 encryption
• Watermarks
• JPEG pages, or all pages as one long image
• Share, save to Downloads, save to Photos or print

ORGANIZE AND PROTECT
• Folders, favorites and search
• Reorder, merge and split documents
• App lock with a PIN, fingerprint or Face ID
• Light and dark themes
